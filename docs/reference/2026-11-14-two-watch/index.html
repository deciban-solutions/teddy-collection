Teddy Baldassarre · two-watch collection challenge

Two-Watch Challenge Lineup

Six watch enthusiasts each picked a two-watch collection meant to cover every wearing occasion. Three store staff then ranked the six pairs without knowing who picked which. The pairs are listed below from first place to sixth, with a photo and source link for every watch.

How each watch was identifiedThe video puts a labelled card of each pair on screen during the recap (36:30–41:30). The photos here are crops of those cards. The picker's own words in the transcript agree with every card.
How the ranking was readFrom the transcript of the reveal (43:30–48:40), where the judges announce the pairs from last place to first.
How links were checkedEvery link below was opened and its title or reference matched. IWC blocks automated visits, so I confirmed its references through IWC's own press releases instead.
